# Carport package rates

Reviewed September 19, 2026. New quotes use covered width × depth. Saved legacy quotes retain their old pricing.

Texas Metal Experts in Burleson publishes $25–$40/sq ft for attached lean-to covers: https://texasmetalexperts.com/faq/ . This adjacent product benchmark is not an identical freestanding CJM quote. The editable $25/sq ft starting rate covers an open square-tube frame, corrugated roof, standard finish, installation and base plates on suitable existing concrete at 9 ft height.

The $4,500 minimum, 30% target margin, $25/post-foot height upgrade, $10/sq ft side panels, $10/ft gutters, $350/footing and $6/roof-sq-ft standing-seam upgrade are CJM planning assumptions, not published competitor bids. Concrete starts at $8.75/sq ft from the supplied CJM large-job guide. Confirm costs and site conditions. Rigid frames, attachments, pipe, polycarbonate and special coatings require a custom allowance.

Rates already include installation and selling margin; legacy markup and labor are not added again. Zero site allowances are excluded. Internal costs cover materials/waste, labor, equipment, transport, permits, engineering, subcontractors/concrete, site work and overhead. Initial materials/labor use the cost price book; other costs require review.

Minimum pretax price = max(job minimum, cost / (1 - target margin)). Discounts cannot reduce the price below this floor. Tax follows. Cost review expires when relevant design or pricing changes. Unreviewed packages can be saved but cannot be issued. Internal costs are not printed on customer documents.
